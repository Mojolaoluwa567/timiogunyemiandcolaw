BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

A few things people usually ask first.

Don't see your question here? A first conversation costs nothing — reach out and we'll walk through it directly.

Do you work with early-stage startups, or only established companies?

Both. A significant part of our practice is startup and VC advisory formation, cap tables, term sheets and fundraising rounds alongside corporate and institutional clients. We scale our involvement to match where you actually are, not a one-size-fits-all retainer.

Can you handle matters that involve other countries, not just Nigeria?

Yes. Several of our matters involve cross-border structuring, licensing, or transactions with counsel or counterparties outside Nigeria. We'll tell you upfront if a matter needs additional foreign counsel, and coordinate directly with them where required.

What actually happens in a first consultation?

You tell us where things stand and what you're trying to achieve. We ask the questions needed to understand the real problem not just the legal question on the surface — and give you a clear sense of next steps, likely scope, and cost before any engagement begins.

How is pricing structured — hourly, flat fee, or something else?

It depends on the matter. Transactional and advisory work is often quoted as a flat fee once scope is clear; litigation and open-ended matters are usually hourly or milestone-based. We agree on structure and cost with you before work begins — no surprises on the invoice.
