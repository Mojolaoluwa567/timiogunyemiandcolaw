Background

Prof. Ogunyemi was called to the Nigerian Bar in 2007. Across nearly two decades of practice, he has built a depth of legal knowledge and professional judgment that now informs the firm's most demanding work, from strategy through to execution.

As Of Counsel, he works alongside the founding partner and the wider team, offering seasoned guidance on complex matters and helping clients see the clearest path through them. His view is that depth of experience is only useful when it's shared.
