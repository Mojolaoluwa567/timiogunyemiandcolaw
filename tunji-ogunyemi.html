Biography

Adetunji O. Ogunyemi is a Lawyer and Advocate of the Supreme Court of Nigeria, with extensive academic and professional experience spanning Law, Constitutional Law, Public Finance, Budget History, Economic History and Labour Relations.

He is a Professor of Economic History at the Department of History, Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, where he has also served as Head of Department (HOD). His multidisciplinary practice and scholarship combine legal analysis, historical research and expertise in public finance, with particular emphasis on the legal and institutional frameworks governing budgeting, fiscal accountability, public financial management and legislative oversight.

Professor Ogunyemi holds a PhD in Public Finance and Budget History from the University of Lagos. His doctoral research, titled Federal Budgets in Nigeria, 1954–1999: A History of the Processes, Policies and Problems, examined the development, processes, policies and challenges of federal budgeting in Nigeria.

His scholarship extends across Economic History, Constitutional Law, Public Finance and Labour Relations. He has published 82 articles in refereed national and international journals and is the author and editor of several books on Economic History, Biographical Studies and the History of the Poor in Africa.

Professor Ogunyemi has provided academic and professional expertise to government institutions, legislative bodies and international organisations on matters relating to public accountability, budgeting, fiscal prudence, legislative oversight, constitutional governance and the legal framework for planning and public finance.

Since 2013, he has served as a consultant to the Konrad Adenauer Stiftung, contributing to the training of Nigerian State Legislators on public accountability, particularly budgetary accountability, fiscal prudence and legislative oversight. In this capacity, he has facilitated legislative training programmes across 22 States of the Federation.

He has also provided technical expertise to the Federal Ministry of Budget and Planning and the Nigerian Senate Committee on National Planning and Economic Affairs on the legal and regulatory framework governing planning, budgeting and economic affairs in Nigeria.

His professional and academic engagements extend beyond Nigeria. In 2022, he served as Visiting Professor of Economic History at the University of The Gambia, Kanifing, The Gambia, at the invitation of the Gambian Ministry of Higher Education, Research, Science and Technology. He has also been invited as Visiting Professor of Constitutional Law by the University of The Gambia.

Professor Ogunyemi is a member of the Nigerian Bar Association and the West Africa Bar Association. He is also a member and Secretary of the Organisation for Historical Research in Nigeria and a Life Member of the Historical Society of Nigeria.

Relevant experience

Constitutional Law & Legal Practice

Practises and conducts scholarly work in Constitutional Law, with particular interests in constitutional governance, separation of powers, legislative powers, public accountability and the legal framework governing public institutions.

His legal background complements his expertise in public finance and governance, enabling him to examine public-sector and legislative issues from both legal and historical perspectives.

  • Served as a facilitator at the Induction Programme for Members-Elect of the 10th National Assembly in May 2023, where he presented a paper titled “Parliamentary Immunities, Privileges and Code of Conduct.”
  • Provides academic and professional input on constitutional and institutional questions relating to governance, public administration and legislative affairs.

Public Finance & Budgeting

Specialises in the history, legal framework and institutional development of public finance and budgeting in Nigeria and Africa.

  • His doctoral research focused specifically on the history of federal budgeting in Nigeria from 1954 to 1999, examining the processes, policies and challenges associated with federal budgets.
  • Has provided expertise on budgetary accountability, fiscal prudence, public financial management and legislative oversight.
  • Since 2013, has served as a consultant to the Konrad Adenauer Stiftung for programmes involving the training of Nigerian legislators on public accountability and budgetary oversight.
  • Has facilitated legislative training programmes across 22 States of the Federation, including Ogun, Oyo, Lagos, Ondo, Delta, Cross River, Akwa Ibom, Enugu, Anambra, Plateau, Bauchi, Sokoto, Kaduna and Taraba States.

Legislative Affairs & Public Accountability

Has extensive experience in legislative capacity-building, particularly in relation to public accountability, fiscal responsibility, budgetary oversight and constitutional governance.

  • In December 2019, served as Chair of the Technical Session of the Federal Ministry of Budget and Planning on “The Legal and Regulatory Environment for Planning, Budgeting and Economic Affairs in Nigeria.”
  • In the same month, provided technical input and assistance to the Nigerian Senate Committee on National Planning and Economic Affairs on “The Legal Framework of Planning in Nigeria since 1945.”
  • Facilitated the training of members-elect of the Osun State House of Assembly on the Code of Conduct for Public Officers, Fiscal Accountability and Separation of Powers under the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, 1999 (as altered).

Economic History & Academic Research

Professor Ogunyemi is an Economic Historian whose research focuses particularly on public finance, budgeting and the economic and institutional history of Nigeria and Africa.

He has published 82 articles in refereed national and international journals, including the Inns Law Review, Romanian Historical Yearbook, Revista de Historia Actual, Transactions of the Historical Society of Ghana, West Bohemian Historical Review, African Journal of Economy and Society, Journal of Asian and African Studies, Lagos Historical Review, Lagos Notes and Records and the Journal of the Historical Society of Nigeria.

He is the author and editor of several books covering Economic History, Biographical Studies and the History of the Poor in Africa.

Academic Leadership & Teaching

  • Began his academic career at the University of Lagos in 2001 as a Graduate Assistant.
  • Subsequently joined the Department of History at Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, where he teaches courses including Precolonial Economic History of Africa, Economic History of Nigeria, Economic History of West Africa, Labour Relations in Nigeria and the History of Strategic Thought.
  • Has served as Head of the Department of History, Obafemi Awolowo University, providing academic and administrative leadership to the Department.
  • Serves as an Adjunct Professor of Law at Joseph Ayo Babalola University, Ikeji-Arakeji, Osun State.
  • Currently serves as External Examiner to the Department of Peace and Conflict Studies, Faculty of Multidisciplinary Studies, University of Ibadan.

International Academic Engagement

  • In 2022, served as Visiting Professor of Economic History at the University of The Gambia, Kanifing, The Gambia.
  • Has also been invited as Visiting Professor of Constitutional Law by the University of The Gambia.

Awards & distinctions

Best PhD Thesis Award — University of Lagos

Professor Ogunyemi's doctoral thesis, Federal Budgets in Nigeria, 1954–1999: A History of the Processes, Policies and Problems, submitted to the University of Lagos in 2008, received the Best PhD Thesis Award across the Faculties of Arts, Business Administration, Social Sciences and Law of the University of Lagos.

National Universities Commission Best PhD Thesis Award

The same doctoral research received the National Universities Commission (NUC) Award for Best PhD Thesis in the Discipline of Arts in Nigerian Universities for 2008.
