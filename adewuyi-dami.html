Biography

Damilola Adewuyi is a Legal Associate at Timi Ogunyemi & Co., with professional interests spanning Corporate and Commercial Law, Dispute Resolution, Property and Real Estate Law, Intellectual Property Law, and Regulatory Compliance.

He holds a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) from Obafemi Awolowo University and a Bachelor of Laws (B.L.) from the Nigerian Law School, Kano Campus. He is a member of the Nigerian Bar Association and is licensed to practise law in Nigeria.

Damilola has developed experience in legal research, commercial advisory, regulatory compliance, contract review, and general legal practice. Prior to joining the firm, he gained practical legal experience through internships with established Nigerian law firms, including Templars and Olufemi Akintomiwa & Co., where he was involved in legal research, regulatory compliance and other aspects of legal advisory work.

His professional interests include corporate and commercial transactions, dispute resolution, property and real estate matters, intellectual property, and regulatory compliance. He approaches legal work with a focus on careful research, sound legal analysis, and practical solutions that address clients' legal and commercial needs.

Beyond his legal practice, Damilola is committed to community service and has a strong interest in initiatives aimed at supporting disadvantaged individuals and contributing positively to his community.
