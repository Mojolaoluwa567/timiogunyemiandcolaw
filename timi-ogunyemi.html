Background

Oluwatimilehin has advised financial institutions, companies, entrepreneurs, and individuals on commercial transactions, contractual arrangements, regulatory obligations, corporate governance, legal risk, and dispute resolution. His transactional experience includes drafting, reviewing, and negotiating a broad range of commercial agreements and advising clients on the legal and regulatory implications of their business relationships.

He has also handled complex banking and financial-services matters involving litigation, arbitration, court orders, account restrictions, regulatory investigations, and claims arising from banking relationships — working with internal business teams, regulators, external counsel, and other stakeholders to identify legal risks and develop commercially practical solutions.

His corporate and commercial practice is complemented by experience in regulatory compliance, fintech, taxation, and U.S. immigration law, including matters involving VAWA self-petitions, T Visas, U Visas, and Forms I-360 and I-485 — among them the preparation of detailed legal arguments and responses to Requests for Evidence. He brings a commercially focused approach to legal practice, combining careful legal analysis with an understanding of the commercial, regulatory, and operational considerations affecting clients.
